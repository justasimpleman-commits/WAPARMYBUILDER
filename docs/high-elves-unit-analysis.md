## Method & assumptions

Scope: **Core, Special and Rare only** — no characters, no war machines (Eagle Claw
Bolt Thrower and the Skycutter's Eagle Eye upgrade are excluded).

- Weapon maths follows 9th Ed 3.0 `rules-common.js`: **great weapon = S+1 with
  Mighty Blow (1)**, so first-round S+2 and sustained S+1 (hence the split builds).
  **Polearm = S+1 permanently.** Light lance = Mighty Blow (1) on the charge only,
  heavy lance = Mighty Blow (2) on the charge only.
- **Elven Grace (Dodge 6+) is deliberately not encoded as a ward.** It is melee-only
  and does not work against Always Strikes First. Every Elf model in the book has it,
  so it multiplies melee resilience by ~1.2 for everything except the War Lions,
  Great Eagle, Merwyrm, Drake and the Phoenixes.
- **Dragon armour's Magical Ward (6+)** and the Phoenix's **Attuned to Magic
  Magical Ward (5+)** only apply to magical attacks and are excluded.
- **Lion cloak / Lion Coat Natural Armour (6+)** is missile-only and excluded from
  the armour save.
- Multi-profile models (cavalry, chariots, the Drakemaster, the Merwyrm's tail) are
  split into component builds **at the same points cost**, so their damage rows can
  simply be **added** to get the model's total output per 100 pts.
- Chariot Impact Hits are modelled as **D6 (avg 3.5) hits at the chariot's Strength 5**
  — a stated assumption, not a book quote.
- Phoenix Guard are Rare unless Finubar is taken; Sisters of Avelorn are Rare unless
  Alarielle is taken.

- **Hold** is the probability the unit passes a representative Break test after
  losing a round of combat — `2d6 <= Ld`, taken at Ld-2 for a plain unit and at
  full Ld if Stubborn. **ADJ = AVG x Hold** is shown as a
  trailing reference column only — it does not feed into AVG and does not affect
  the ordering, so the headline number stays a pure durability-per-point figure.
  No general's Inspiring Presence bubble is applied; a Ld10 general in
  range flattens almost every unit here to a 0.72 hold.

- The **Drakemaster's 2+ save** combines the rider's dragon armour (+3), shield (+1)
  and the Drake's Natural Armour 4+ (+3) into one save for the model. If the Drake
  instead saves on its own Natural Armour only (4+), its AVG drops from 32.0 to
  roughly 14. This is the shakiest number in the report.
