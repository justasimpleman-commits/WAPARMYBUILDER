# Daemons of Chaos — CHARACTER combat analysis

Characters only: generic lords and heroes plus every special character. The
Core/Special/Rare units are in `daemons-of-chaos-unit-analysis.md`.

**All characters are costed BARE** — no magic items, no Daemonic Gifts, except items
printed on a special character's own profile. Generic lords carry 50–100 pts of budget
that is not reflected here, so treat their rows as a floor.

## Read both views

Per-point normalisation is the right lens for rank-and-file, where you choose *how
many*. For characters you choose *whether*, so this report gives each metric twice:

- **Per 100 pts** — is this model efficient? Answers "am I overpaying".
- **Absolute** — how tough / how killy is it, full stop? Answers "will this lord win
  the duel". These tables are not divided by cost and rank very differently.

**Hold / ADJ** = Daemonic Instability reliability, informational only — excluded from
the average and the ordering. Daemons are Unbreakable but a lost round means a Break
test (`2d6 ≤ Ld − loss margin`) with one unsaveable Wound per point failed. `Hold` is
`P(2d6 ≤ Ld − 2)` — the chance a 2-point loss costs nothing extra. Unlike the rank-and-
file report this column actually discriminates here, because character Leadership runs
7 to 10:

| Ld | Hold | Who |
|----|------|-----|
| 10 | 0.72 | Be'lakor, Azazel |
| 9 | 0.58 | the greater daemons, Daemon Prince, most special characters |
| 8 | 0.42 | heroes (Bloodmaster, Poxbringer, Viceleader, Changecaster, Skulltaker, Karanak) |
| 7 | 0.28 | Skaarac (Loner), The Blue Scribes |

## Method & assumptions

Scope: **characters only.** Source: `data/daemons-of-chaos.js`, verified against
`reference/Army books/Warhammer - Daemons of Chaos 3.0.pdf` and the 9th Ed 3.11
rulebook. Shared modelling conventions are documented in
`daemons-of-chaos-unit-analysis.md`; what follows is character-specific.

**Builds chosen**

- Daemon Prince / Exalted Daemon: **medium armour** (the best armour they may buy,
  +15 pts) for resilience; bare profile for damage. Their alignment upgrade (+15) and
  Fly (+25) are not costed.
- Bloodthirster: both **two hand weapons** (+1 Attack, its default) and the free
  **great weapon** swap, first round and sustained.
- Great Unclean One: **additional hand weapon** (+20) for the damage row.
- Bloodmaster: **great weapon + light armour**.
- Wizard levels are not bought — a Level 4 upgrade costs 105–140 pts and buys no
  combat stats, so including it would only distort the per-point columns.

**Magic weapons printed on profiles**

Modelled as Ignores Armour Saves where the book says so: Be'lakor (Blade of Shadows),
Skarbrand (Slaughter and Carnage, also two hand weapons), Azazel (Daemonblade). Skarbrand's
**Frenzy** (+1 Attack) and **Hatred** are included. Shalaxi's Soulpiercer is a spear,
which grants no Strength bonus outside being charged, so she is modelled at base S6.

**Not modelled**

- **Every Daemonic Gift.** Several are large combat swings — Dark Insanity (Random
  Attacks 2D6+2), Slaughterborn (+D3 Attacks), Unholy Flurry, Massive Might. A 100-pt
  Bloodthirster gift load can move its damage row by 30–50%.
- **Killing Blow** (Bloodthirster, Bloodmaster, Skarbrand, Skulltaker) and **Heroic
  Killing Blow** (Skulltaker and Shalaxi in challenges). This materially understates
  the Khorne duellists.
- **Look Out Sir**, unit-joining, and challenge mechanics generally.
- Wound-restoring rules (Life Eater, Spirit Swallower, Souleater, Crimson Soulstone),
  which raise effective durability beyond the printed Wounds.
- Aura and debuff rules (The Dark Master, Aura of Slaanesh, Willing Prey, Soporific
  Musk, The Tally of Pestilence, Formless Horror). Several characters — the Masque,
  the Changeling, Epidemius, the Blue Scribes, Kairos — score badly here precisely
  because their entire value is in rules this analysis does not measure.
- Monstrous Creatures use **Stomp (D3)** = 2 hits, Monsters **Stomp (D6)** = 3.5,
  applied only against targets with Line of Sight ≤ 3.
